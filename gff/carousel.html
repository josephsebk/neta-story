The Setup

How do you find out what a conference really talks about?

758
Transcripts parsed.English auto-captions, cleaned, word-counted and dated to the right edition.
27
Themes counted.Measured per 10,000 words — so a long keynote and a short panel count on the same scale.

Four years, one method — so the editions can actually be compared against each other.
